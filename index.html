Chrome extension · v0.3.0 · No account required

From quick thought to saved note.

Quick notes
Press Alt + Q (Option + Q on Mac) to jot something down on the page you’re viewing. Reopen saved notes in Chrome’s side panel.
Screenshots
Paste a screenshot or image from your clipboard into a note. Open the saved attachment for a closer look.
Saved tabs
Select several Chrome tabs and attach them to one note. Reopen the links when you’re ready to pick up where you left off.
Calendar
Write a date or time in a note to see it as an event in the built-in calendar. Select the date to review the event and return to the note.

Optional Google Drive sync

In development · Unavailable in v0.3.0

Notes are saved on your device. When Drive sync is released, you’ll be able to connect a Google account and sync notes, images, and saved tabs across devices. Sync will use Alt+Q Notes’ app data folder in your Drive.
