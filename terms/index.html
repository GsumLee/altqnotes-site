Using Alt+Q Notes

You can use the extension to create notes, paste images, save links, and view dated entries in a calendar. Use the extension lawfully and save or share only content you have permission to use.

Your content

You retain your rights in the content you add. The extension stores your content in your browser profile and saves synced copies in your Google Drive if you connect sync. Removing the extension or clearing the profile can remove locally stored data, so keep a separate copy of anything you need to retain.

Google Drive sync

Optional Google Drive sync is available in v0.4.0 and later. To use it, connect your Google account and approve access to Alt+Q Notes’ app-specific Drive data. Sync covers saved notes, images, saved tab links, and Trash. Logging out of Drive sync stops syncing from that browser and retains existing copies on the device and in Drive. Your use of Google Drive is also subject to Google’s terms. The Privacy Policy explains what sync stores and how to remove the Drive data.
